PDF Encryption and Decryption

Add password protection to a PDF, or remove its password by recreating an unrestricted PDF. Runs locally in the browser for privacy.

Click or drag to upload PDF file

- -

After encryption, anyone opening this PDF will need the password. Be sure to remember it!

After decryption, a new unrestricted PDF will be generated and exported for easy sharing and printing.
