Split PDF

Split by individual page, specify page ranges, or extract selected pages with secure offline browser-based processing.

Click or drag to upload a PDF file

- -

Enter page numbers or ranges to extract（Starting from page 1）。Separate each segment with a semicolon or comma. For example, 1-3, 5 creates two PDF files: pages 1-3 and page 5.

For a 10-page PDF, setting this to 2 creates 5 PDFs (pages 1-2, 3-4, and so on).
